NARRATIVE RADAR - Listening Between the Lies

The only things you need in this folder:

  Start Narrative Radar   Double-click to start. Leave the black window open.
  Open Radar              Opens the Radar page (http://localhost:8765).
  Install 24-7            One time: makes the Radar start every time you log in.
  Remove 24-7             Undoes that.
  watchlist               Your topics, one per line. You can also add them on the Radar page.
  Narrative-Radar-Guide   Picture guide: what every part of the Radar means.

Folders (you never need to open these):

  engine            The Radar program and its saved data. Don't delete it.
  deep-dive tools   The original tools for studying one topic over 14 days.

Full manual: the "Narrative Radar Master Guide" in your Claude chat.
